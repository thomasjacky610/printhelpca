Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Advertisement

PrintWise Canada is an independent informational website. This policy explains what information we collect from visitors and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Contact form submissions — name, email address, province/territory, and message content when you write to us
  • Analytics data — pages visited, session duration, browser type, and device type, collected via analytics software to help us improve the site
  • Cookies — for site functionality, analytics, and advertising as described below

We do not collect payment information, government identification, or any sensitive personal data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form
  • To understand how Canadian visitors use the site and improve our content
  • To display relevant advertising through our third-party advertising partner

Cookies and Advertising

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. Our advertising partner may use cookies to serve advertising that reflects your interests based on prior browsing behaviour. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or through any consent tool present on the site.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with our hosting provider and advertising partner, both operating under appropriate data agreements. We do not share personal information with printer manufacturers, retailers, or any other third party for marketing purposes.

Canadian Privacy Law

PrintWise operates in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation. Canadian residents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data held by us.

Your Rights

To exercise any of your privacy rights, email us at privacy@printwise.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Continued use of the site following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Privacy enquiries: privacy@printwise.ca
