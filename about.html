Who We Are

PrintWise — Built for Canadian Consumers

PrintWise is an independent informational website dedicated to helping Canadians make confident, well-informed printer purchasing decisions. We have no affiliation with any printer manufacturer, ink or toner supplier, or retailer. We don't earn commissions from sales referrals and we don't accept payment to feature any product or brand.

We built PrintWise because buying a printer in Canada is more confusing than it should be. Retail environments don't give you time to understand the technology. Manufacturer websites are designed to promote their own products. Review sites often focus on specs without explaining what those specs actually mean for everyday use.

Our goal is to give Canadian readers — whether in Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, Quebec, or any other province or territory — the background knowledge to walk into a store or browse online with confidence, knowing what type of printer fits their situation, what specifications matter, and what to watch out for.

Our Approach

What We Do and Don't Do

We explain printer technology in plain language without assuming prior knowledge. We cover real-world considerations — like how cold Canadian winters affect inkjet ink, or why buying a printer with a Canadian warranty matters — that generic international review sites overlook.

We don't recommend specific models or brand names. We don't have affiliate links to retailers. We don't take money to make one type of printer sound better than another. Our content is written to be useful regardless of which specific printer you ultimately choose.

This site is supported by advertising displayed through a third-party platform. Ads appear in clearly labelled positions. Advertising relationships have no influence on our editorial content.
